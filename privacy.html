Privacy Policy

Charted Money uploader ("YoutubeAutopilot"). Last updated: October 26, 2026

YoutubeAutopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Charted Money" to upload the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What data the app accesses

The app requests permission to upload videos and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access, collect or store data about any other user.

How data is used

The authorization token is used only to upload videos, thumbnails, titles and descriptions to the Charted Money channel. It is stored privately as an encrypted secret and is never shared with third parties.

Data sharing

No personal data is sold, shared or transferred to anyone.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
